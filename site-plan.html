WDD 231 · Chamber project

A stronger local business community starts with connection.

A planning brief for a proposed digital resource serving businesses and the wider community in Germiston, Gauteng, South Africa.

01 · Project foundation

Site name and purpose

Germiston Chamber of Commerce

This is a proposed chamber website concept for the WDD 231 course project. It will help local businesses become more visible, find practical business resources, and connect with opportunities in Germiston and the surrounding Ekurhuleni area.

Site goals

  • Make local member businesses easier to discover through a searchable directory.
  • Share useful community, business, and networking event information.
  • Explain the value and process of chamber membership to prospective members.
  • Present Germiston as a place to do business, work, and invest.

02 · People and needs

Target market and personas

The primary audience is Germiston-area business owners and managers. Secondary audiences include residents looking for local services, people planning events, and organizations exploring the area.

The independent owner

Thandi, 34, runs a small service business. She wants affordable ways to promote her business, meet other owners, and learn about local support.

The growing employer

Michael, 46, manages a growing company. He is looking for qualified local suppliers, partnership opportunities, and a stronger presence in the business community.

The new resident

Leila, 29, has recently moved to Germiston. She wants to find trusted nearby services and learn about local events and community organizations.

03 · User scenarios

Questions the site should answer

  1. How can I join, what does membership offer, and who can I contact?
  2. Which local businesses provide the service or product I need?
  3. What business or networking events are happening soon?
  4. Where can I learn about Germiston and opportunities in the area?

04 · Information architecture

Site map

  • Home
    • Discover Germiston
    • Business Directory
    • Join the Chamber

The home page will introduce the chamber and highlight calls to action, events, and featured businesses. Discover will provide community and business-area information. The directory will present member listings. Join will explain membership options and provide an application path.

05 · Page layout

Home page wireframe notes

Desktop

  1. Header with chamber identity and horizontal navigation.
  2. Wide hero image with a clear Join call to action.
  3. Three-column area for events, weather, and announcements.
  4. Member spotlights followed by a full-width footer.

Mobile

  1. Compact header with a menu button and chamber identity.
  2. Full-width hero and prominent Join call to action.
  3. Events, weather, announcements, and member cards stacked vertically.
  4. Readable single-column footer links and contact details.

06 · Findability

SEO plan

  • Use descriptive page titles, headings, and summaries that mention Germiston and the page topic.
  • Write useful, original descriptions for member listings and community information.
  • Use descriptive image alt text, accessible links, and semantic page structure.
  • Invite member organizations to link to their chamber directory listings.
  • Measure page usage with a privacy-conscious analytics setup if one is approved for the project.

07 · Visual direction

Design brief

Primary
#183C36 — deep evergreen
Secondary
#D6A64F — warm gold
Background
#F3F5F1 — soft neutral
Text
#172A26 — dark green-black
Typography
DM Sans, with a sans-serif fallback for clear, approachable reading.

Evergreen establishes a steady civic identity, with gold reserved for highlights and calls to action. Light backgrounds and dark text keep content readable across screen sizes.
